Tips & Tricks

Using Images from your Roll20 Art Library

  1. Place the image on the tabletop.
  2. Select the image and press Z.
  3. Right-click the enlarged image and copy its image URL.
  4. Paste the URL into the Image URL field.

Use Show Image to post the image in the chat. Clear the URL field to remove the image from the sheet.

Combining Quotes and Images in Macros

You can combine character dialogue, descriptions and images in a single Roll20 macro.

/me : "Let me show you something..."
%{selected|repeating_gear_$0_showimage}

Available Image Macro References

Main%{selected|showimage}
Amps%{selected|repeating_amps_$0_showimage}
Weapons%{selected|repeating_combat_$0_showimage}
Vehicles%{selected|repeating_vehicles_$0_showimage}
Contacts%{selected|repeating_contacts_$0_showimage}
Gear%{selected|repeating_gear_$0_showimage}

Replace $0 with $1, $2, etc. to reference subsequent entries.

Replace selected with the character’s name to use the macro without selecting a token.

Reordering or deleting list entries may change which image is referenced by a positional macro.
