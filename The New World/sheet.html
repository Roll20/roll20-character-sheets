Each cap talent raises the roll limit (characteristic + rank) from 9 to 10 for that characteristic's skills.

How rolls work

  • Click a Current characteristic, a skill Total or a skill name to roll 2d6 + characteristic + rank against a difficulty.
  • Mark skill ranks in the boxes: −2 / −1 untrained (advanced / basic), 0 Familiar, +1 Trained, +2 Expert, +3 Master. Totals are capped at the roll limit.
  • Click the die beside a weapon to attack: enter the target's Defence, and the card shows the hit, margin, location and damage before Endurance and armour. Fatigue is taken off attacks and casting.
  • A weapon uses the skill whose group (the line in brackets) matches the weapon's Group, e.g. write Fencing beside Melee and on the smallsword.
  • Snake eyes always fails. Doubles on a hit are a critical: double weapon Damage.
  • Spend Luck after the dice with the Spend button by the Luck box, or the link on the roll card.
