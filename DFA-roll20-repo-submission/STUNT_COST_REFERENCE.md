# Stunt Cost Reference

General pricing guidelines for homebrewing Stunts on the `repeating_stunts` section
of the sheet. These are guidelines, not hard rules — the GM has final say. Every
Stunt should be tied to a specific skill and should feel like "two shifts" of
narrative benefit, not a blanket rewrite of the rules.

| Refresh Cost | Scope | Examples of scope (not specific stunts) |
|---|---|---|
| **0** (rare, GM discretion) | A very narrow reskin of something the skill could already sort-of do; usually folded into character concept instead of costed | Reflavoring an existing trapping to fit your theme |
| **-1** (most common) | A single, focused bonus: +2 to a skill in a narrow circumstance, a new use for a skill, or a minor situational ability | Most mortal stunts fall here |
| **-2** | A broader bonus (+2 in a wide circumstance, or +1 in a broad one), a stunt with a resource cost (once per session), or one with a real drawback if it fails | Stunts that grant a recurring resource, minor supernatural-flavored tricks |
| **-3 or more** | Stacks multiple narrower stunts into one package, grants a repeatable powerful effect, or is really a small Power in disguise | Cohorts, signature magic items with real combat impact, minor supernatural powers |

**Rules of thumb**
- A Stunt should reference the skill it modifies by name in its title or text — this is what the `Linked Skill` column is for.
- If a Stunt would let a skill replace another skill's core use case entirely, price it like the more expensive skill's territory, not the cheaper one.
- Powers (real supernatural abilities — Inhuman Strength, Sponsored Magic, etc.) are usually priced by their template rather than by this table; treat this table as being for stunts layered on top of mundane or lightly-supernatural skill use.
- The sheet's Vitals panel auto-sums every row's Refresh Cost against your Power Level's Base Refresh to get Adjusted Refresh and starting Fate Points — so cost each Stunt honestly rather than working backward from a Refresh total you want to land on.
