Character Creation Wizard

Follow these steps in order. Fill each section, then continue to the next.
All values entered here sync with the main sheet tabs.

Step 1 — Choose Your Concept

In AD&D 2E, you first imagine the character, then pick the mechanics to match. What kind of hero are you? A wandering sellsword? A cloistered mage? A roguish entertainer?

Step 2 — Roll & Assign Ability Scores

Roll 4d6, drop the lowest die, six times. Or roll 3d6 in order. Assign the results to the six abilities according to your class's prime requisites. Click the roll buttons below to roll in chat, then type the result.

 
Step 3 — Roll Starting Money

See Table 43 in the PHB. Warriors: 5d4×10 gp · Wizards: (1d4+1)×10 gp · Rogues: 2d6×10 gp · Priests: 3d6×10 gp
Note: Priests may only use starting funds to buy equipment — remaining gold must be returned.

     
Step 4 — Roll Hit Points

Warriors d10 · Priests/Rangers d8 · Rogues/Bards d6 · Wizards d4. Add CON bonus. At 1st level, some DMs allow maximum HP.

     
Step 5 — Set THAC0 & Saving Throws

Look up your class and level in PHB Tables 53–54. Warriors begin at THAC0 20, improving 1/level. Priests/Rogues improve every 2 levels. Wizards every 3 levels.

Step 6 — Choose Proficiencies

Warriors start with 4 WPs and 3 NWPs. Priests 2 WPs / 4 NWPs. Rogues 3 WPs / 3 NWPs. Wizards 1 WP / 4 NWPs. Add the Proficiencies tab to fill in the details. Record your choices here as a summary.

Step 7 — Write Your Background

Where did your character come from? What drives them? What are they afraid of? Even a few sentences will make them memorable at the table. Fill in the Biography tab for the full version.
