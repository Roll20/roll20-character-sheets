LAST WORDS

When you speak your last words, you:

  • Inspire your crew as you perish. Go around the table and ask each crewmate if you were a good captain in the end. If their heart says yes, they mark all-luck; if their heart says no, they may clear all of their troubles.
  • Fall in disgrace, but remain barely alive. Lose everything you had—your captain ship, your dignity, your treasure. Choose a new playbook.

When you speak your last words, you:

  • Perform desperate surgery on yourself and take a trouble: “Barely Alive [die]. You cling to life.” What ghastly mark does it leave on you?
  • Give in with grace (all crewmates who stand vigil for your death heal 1-harm) or without it (describe what terrible medical abomination you leave behind).

Will you speak your last words?

  • Yes, and they are your life’s work. Your last words become a djinni of your own making, sculpted by your final thoughts. Use the djinni as a new character, or let your work be interpreted by the Fates.
  • No, you live by running away. Embrace cowardice: run and leave your friends to their terrible fate. Leave everything behind, including your ship and the sea. Make a new character if you wish to stay on the crew.

When you speak your last words, do you still seek vengeance?

  • Yes, you still have people who need killing. Return from death as a revenant. You no longer eat, drink, or sleep; you take -1 to

    standing your ground

    until you’re given a proper death (or change playbooks). Choose one body part and one memory you’ve lost.
  • No, you are sick of dealing death. Roll 1d6. If you roll a result greater than the amount of marked deeds you currently have, you’ve redeemed yourself; die in peace. Otherwise, you must return.

When you speak your last words, you:

  • Sacrifice yourself to get everyone to safety. Once everyone’s safe, join them and die in the company of your crew.
  • Cling to life in a blind rage. You go too far. Choose something to destroy utterly. The Fates will choose something precious you destroy too.

When you speak your last words, choose one:

  • Explode in a hellish inferno as your body becomes a vessel for your demon. Tell the Fates what the demon does next; after it finishes this final task, it’s in fate’s hands.
  • Make a desperate bargain with your demon for your life. They will ask for something terrible in return.

When you speak your last words, choose one:

  • The Sea saves you. It wraps you in its embrace, protects you from all ills, and then takes you away forever. What are your last words to your crewmates as you’re taken? Make a new character unless or until your Navigator is saved from the depths.
  • You die, and the Sea mourns. Describe the terrible, awe-inspiring thing that the ocean and its servants do to your body and your enemies.

When you speak your last words, you:

  • Ask your nemesis to save you. If they agree, you’re in their debt. If they don’t, then...
  • Go it alone. You’re gone. Secretly, tell the Fates if your character dies now, or comes back as a villain NPC with a terrible grudge against the crew.

When you speak your last words, you may:

  • Get back up and fight until your current enemy has been vanquished. When they’ve been defeated, fall to your knees, reveal the mortal wound you’ve been hiding, and have a dramatic death scene.
  • Barely live, but with a terrible injury that leaves your face marred or your body weak. Choose a new playbook.
TROUBLES
OBSESSED

You can be compelled by the Fates to pursue your ambition at all costs. If you screw over a crewmate in the process, mark 1 experience and 1 unrest. Whenever you clear Obsessed, remove 1 unrest.

ENDPOINT: resist the compel for the sake of the crew.

ENTHRALLED

While Enthralled, the Fates may compel you to do exciting science at the risk of human life. If you put a crewmate on the chopping block to do so, mark 1 experience.

ENDPOINT: resist the compel for the sake of the crew.

BOOK FEVER

You can be compelled by the Fates to retreat into your books to escape problems. When you abandon the needs of the crew while doing so, mark 1 experience and gain 1 bond with the book you’ve retreated to, which now contains a djinni. Spend 1 bond with this djinni to use the book’s powers freely—without rolling, conditions, or consequences. If you wish to use the book otherwise, you must parley with the djinni instead of unleashing its power.

ENDPOINT: face headlong something that scares you.

BLOODTHIRSTY

You are compelled to see anyone between you and your goal as mere obstacles, easily put down. When you kill someone innocent for the sake of vengeance, mark 1 experience.

ENDPOINT: choose not to take vengeance.

Instead of a vice, you have 4 Matelot troubles. When asked to mark trouble, mark the next Matelot trouble on the track, starting from the top and going down.

PISSED.
Compelled to get violent when your temper is set off.

ENDPOINT: take a breather.

RASH.
Compelled to rush in recklessly and thoughtlessly.

ENDPOINT: get into bad trouble.

FURIOUS.
Compelled to smash all your problems.

ENDPOINT: destroy important thing.

RAGING.
Compelled to go into a wild, violent rage; if you do, mark 1 experience.

ENDPOINT: hurt a crewmate

BEDEVILED

While Bedeviled, you are compelled to do as your demon says; if it asks you to do something nasty to a crewmate, do it willingly to mark 1 experience. If you resist the compel, pick from the list of sacrifices or else lose 1 bond with the demon.

ENDPOINT: resist the compel for a crewmate’s sake.

HUNTED

If you break the rule dictated by the sea’s curse, mark all-luck, but you are Hunted; the sea is now searching for you. While Hunted, you can be compelled by the Fates to give in to the sea’s call and walk for the waves; once there, the sea’s servants will rise from it and attempt to claim you. If you give in willingly, and are scarred for it, mark 1 experience.

ENDPOINT: encounter the sea’s servants.

GREEDY

While Greedy, the Fates may compel you to steal something you shouldn’t. If you do it willingly and get the crew in trouble, mark 1 experience.

ENDPOINT: resist for the sake of the crew

IMPASSIONED

While you’re Impassioned, the Fates can compel you to do extremely foolish things to impress your connections. If this gets another of your connections in trouble, mark 1 experience; that connec tion can choose to reject you for it. If they do, lose 1 bond with them and gain 1 bond with another.

ENDPOINT: break a heart.

CHARACTER MOVES BELONGINGS DOWNTIME
UNREST
When the unrest track fills, the crew becomes wild; erase all tallies and start over. If the track fills while all or most of the crew is wild, your lackeys attempt a mutiny; each player character must choose whose side they’re on. Quell the uprising, or speak your Last Words.
PLAYBOOK MOVES
COMMAND.
When you lead a group of your crewmates to accomplish a basic move together, you may roll +POLISH instead of that move’s original trait. As long as you have marks on your unrest track, any involved PC, or any wild NPC, may choose to sabotage this move before you roll it, and give it a -1 forward. If a PC chooses to do so, they mark luck equal to marks on your unrest track. If you roll a miss, mark Obsessed in addition to any other effects; if you’re already Obsessed, mark 1 on the unrest track.
In addition, at character creation, mark one of the following moves.
BLACKHEARTED.
At the beginning of each session, you may mark luck equal to the marks on your unrest track. If you make an example of a mutinous lackey, you may spend 1-luck to replace the wild tags of your lackeys with rattled.
KISS FROM NEPTUNE REX.
When your crew wins a fight under your command, remove a mark on the unrest track or refresh a crewmate’s bonds, your choice.
LAIR.
You have a lair; mark it on the map. Pick two features of your lair:
a size-1 group of loyal lackeys
supply of a rare resource
a business front (take +1-treasure when you spend downtime here)
a locus
tools and a workshop
a secret entrance
lots of rum and entertainment
a skilled ship doctor
When you, or anyone you allow, makes use of the lair’s features during a downtime, they take +1 to the relevant move.
RECRUIT.
When you recruit new talent during downtime while at port, spend 3-treasure and roll +POLISH. On a hit, you’ve recruited a size-1 group of lackeys; choose a tag for them. On a 10+, you make a special connection with one of them; take a bond with a single NPC in the group. On a miss, you’ve only found one wild person with baggage—but they’ve got something on you. Hire them or they’ll cause issues for you and the crew.
MASTERMIND.
When you encounter an obstacle while leading your crew, you may spend a bond with an involved crewmate to explain how you planned for this beforehand, and twist fate as if you’d spent 2 luck.
RUMORS.
When you spread rumors during downtime, roll +POLISH. On a hit, it catches like wildfire. On a 10+, you may give the locals a tag based on your skullduggery, or they’re unaware you’re the source of influence, your choice. On a miss, you make enemies of the targets or a fool of yourself, your choice.
TAKES ONE TO KNOW ONE.
When you read someone, roll +VINEGAR. On a hit, ask 1; on a 10+, ask 2. On a miss, they ask 1 instead. You may spend a bond with them to ask all three, even after rolling.
  • What are your true intentions here?
  • How can I get you to do what I want?
  • What is your achilles’ heel?
SURGERY.
When you give a willing patient surgery, tell the Fates what you want the surgery to do for the patient and pick 1 strange rare ingredient or tool you need for it. The Fates may require you to use 1 to 3 more ingredients or tools, based on the scale of the surgery requested. Once you have everything you need, take an hour with your surgery tools and roll +VINEGAR.
On a hit, it works—choose 1 side effect from the following list. On a 7-9, choose 2.
  • It’s time-consuming; you must expend a downtime to complete it.
  • It’s messy; you exhaust your surgery tools.
  • It’s fascinating; you take Enthralled.
On a miss, the surgery works but goes awry; the patient takes a terrible side effect in the form of a trouble of the Fates’ choice, with the endpoint [remove whatever the surgery did to you, and take 1 harm].
ALCHEMY.
At the beginning of every session, if you have your alchemy tools, you may roll +VINEGAR. On a hit, hold 1. On a 10+, hold 2. On a miss, your alchemy tools have run out; you’ll have to find or buy more. Spend hold, 1-for-1, to have one of the following on hand when you need it. Your hold expires at the end of the session.
  • 3 explosives (2-harm, area, dangerous, loud, fire, works on impact)
  • 3 stinkbombs (area, +1 to Clever Trick, smelly, stun, explodes after five minutes)
  • 1 poison vial (slow, consume: 3-harm paralyze)
  • 1 vial of superglue (dangerous, can stick anything together, weak to salt water)
  • 1 canister of sleeping gas (area sleep, works on impact)
  • 3 smokebombs (area, +1 to Clever Trick, blind, works on impact)
In addition, at character creation, mark one of the following moves.
CLERICAL STUDY.
When you use surgery to heal a crewmate’s harm or remove a physical trouble, you need no strange ingredients or tools. If you hit, choose 1 fewer side effect than you normally would.
HEALING SALVES.
Add to your list of alchemies:
  • 3 Healing Salves (slow, touch: +1 health)
  • 3 Antitoxin (slow, consume: removes poison) and
  • 3 Tonic (slow, consume: removes sickness).
HOMUNCULUS.
When you create a homunculus using your alchemy tools, spend 8 hours, instill it with a dark source of strange power, and roll +VINEGAR. On a hit, you’ve made a strange tough-1 aid [blood] servant; write the name of its owner inside its chest so it’s bound to the owner’s orders. On a 10+, give it another tag of your choice. On a 7-9, your alchemy tools or your dark source of power run out in the process, Fates’ choice. On a miss, the Fates will tell you the last ingredient you need to wake your homunculus; find it to achieve a 7-9 result.
HYPOCRITICAL OATH.
While Enthralled, you add +2 when you help, or subtract -3 when you hinder, a crewmate who has been a patient of your surgery.
MISKATONIC.
When you perform a ritual with weird science, you can use +VINEGAR instead of +SPITFIRE. If you do, use your alchemy tools as a locus, and add “You become Enthralled” to the list of consequence choices.
SABOTEUR.
When you break in or out by using your alchemy tools as an opening, you may roll +VINEGAR instead of +BLOOD.
WORDSMITH.
When you decide to write a book, tell the Fates what the book will be about, and what you want the book to do when you read it aloud. The Fates will give you a quest you must complete before you finish it, selecting from the following list. They may add up to 4 additional quests for more powerful books.
  • You must first experience ______.
  • The book must be made from _______.
  • The ink must be infused with ______.
  • _____ must give the book their ______.
When you finish your book during downtime, roll +SPITFIRE. On a hit, pick one requirement to use the book’s magic; the Fates will fill in the blanks. On a 7-9, pick an additional requirement. On a miss, tell the Fates why the book isn’t what you intended it to be; the Fates will tell you what the book does now. Pick two requirements for it; it’s dangerous.
  • The book can only be used _____ .
  • The book won’t work if _____ .
  • To read it, you must first ______ .
  • Reading it causes ______ to ______ .
To use a book, fulfill its requirements, read it aloud, and unleash the book’s power. It affects everyone who hears and understands the words.
In addition, at character creation, mark one of the following moves.
INSPIRATION.
Once per session, you may spend a bond with a crewmate who’s currently inspiring you to unleash the book’s power freely, exactly as you’d like.
LOREKEEPER.
When you encounter a new land, creature, or phenomenon, spend 1-luck to learn a story you’ve heard about it. This story contains or reveals a secret truth; mark Book Fever to know exactly what it is.
PATHOKINESIS.
Whenever you feel (pick 1):
deep rage
crushing sorrow
trauma-fueled fear
a strong emotion of your choice,
you may ignore the conditions or dangerous tags on a book you’re reading.
SEHNSUCHT.
When a crewmate fulfills—or helps you fulfill—one of your book quests at great personal risk, refresh all bonds.
PERUSE.
When you peruse script of any sort in detail, roll +VINEGAR. On a 10+, ask any two questions whose answers you might reasonably deduce from the writing; on a 7-9, ask one. On a miss, the information you seek is missing or damaged; the Fates will tell you how to find what you wanted to know.
TALL TALES.
When you hoodwink someone with a clever ruse, you can roll +VINEGAR instead of +POLISH.
WORDS WRIT LARGE.
Whenever you create a djinni, whether intentionally or accidentally, choose x additional tags it has: allows you to see out of its eyes; 2-harm close touch; invisible; shapeshifter; summons storms. Whenever you call upon that djinni to help you, roll x six sided dice. If any of the dice roll a 6, the djinni is also wild for the duration of their assistance.
DARK DEED.
When you commit a dark deed that helps you commit your vengeance, choose a deed and roll +BLOOD. On a hit, mark and use the deed you chose. On a 7-9, you’re compelled to do the thing in parentheses too. You can’t use an option that’s already been marked. Every currently marked deed appears on your body as a black mark. When you commit an unprompted act of truly gratuitous cold violence, erase all your marks and start over.
Hit your next target dead in the chest for +3-harm forceful piercing. (Then shoot someone you shouldn’t.)
Make someone small do what you want, whatever you want, by threatening violence. (Then do the violence anyway.)
Escape your current perilous situation, even if it seems impossible. (By endangering someone who didn’t deserve it.)
On a miss, you fall to your darkest urges; you accomplish only the result in parentheses. At any time, even after rolling, you may instead choose to destroy everything in your way, no matter friend or foe—but you are then compelled to lose your mind, and retire this character for the Fates to play.
In addition, at character creation, mark one of the following moves.
CALLED SHOT.
When you get into a scrap, you can throw yourself into a precarious position to make a called shot. If you hit, choose a tag from the following options; your target takes that tag in addition to any harm you inflict.
  • Blinded. They can’t see anything.
  • Limping. They can’t walk faster than a slow limp.
  • Maimed. They drop what they’re holding and can’t use that arm.
  • Rattled. They get sloppy. You take +1 on moves against them.
DIRTY DEEDS.
Add these to your list of dark deeds:
Declare someone your target. You can always sense where they are and you take +1 to violence against them. (Take -1 to anything else.)
When you’ve just taken a life, remove all your harm. (Then mutilate their corpse.)
HIGHWAY TO HELL.
Spend 3-luck to use +your current marked deeds as every move’s trait for a scene. If you do, at the end of the scene, mark Bloodthirsty.
KISS FROM A ROSE.
At the end of every session, mark 1-luck if you killed someone. If you currently have all of your deeds marked, mark all-luck instead.
PURPLE HAZE.
When you give into your brooding vices over downtime, describe what den of debauchery you disappear into and roll +your black marks. On a hit, your self-immolation leaves you feeling cleansed; erase two marked deeds. On a 10+, erase a third marked deed or an appropriate trouble, your choice. On a miss, your brooding turns to violence; pick one unwanted attention from testing renown.
STAIRWAY TO HEAVEN.
If you reveal a piece of your tragic backstory to your companion in confidence and they show you genuine sympathy, unmark one of your deeds or refresh all your bonds with them, your choice.
YOU’RE GONNA CARRY THAT WEIGHT.
Mark one of your dark deeds (without using it) to take +3 to standing your ground for a scene.
FISTICUFFS.
Your fists are 2-harm close. When you bust through an obstacle, take a Matelot trouble if you can and roll +BLOOD. On a hit, you destroy the obstacle. On a 10+, pick one; on a 7-9, both occur.
  • You attract unwanted attention.
  • Take 1 harm in the process.
On a miss, you wreck something you shouldn’t have. The Fates will tell you how you’ve made new obstacles for yourself.
HEART OF GOLD.
At character creation, mark two of the options. Do what it says and spend 1 bond with your crewmate to accomplish those effects.
Lend them all of your might, and add 2 to their roll (you may do this before or after rolling).
Dive in front of them and suffer the consequence the Fates inflicted on them (harm, trouble, etc.)
Provide them a steady hand or word, and give them a 10+ while standing their ground.
Look into their eyes and know exactly what they’re up to.
In addition, at character creation, mark one of the following moves.
CUMSHAW KING.
When you come to harbor, roll +POLISH. On a hit, you know a friendly face; the Fates decide who it is. Decide if they offer a gift or useful info. On a 10+, gain 1 bond or refresh bonds with them as well. On a miss, a local friend is missing, dead, or in dire straits. The Fates will tell you what happened; take +1 forward to acting on it.
DUTY-BOUND.
Mark the two remaining options for your heart of gold move. You may spend 2-luck instead of 1 bond to use your heart of gold effects on crewmates.
FRIENDS WITH MERMAIDS.
You are friends with all the sea’s creatures; you can speak with and be understood by them. When you submerge yourself fully in water, you can unleash your power to summon the sea’s wild servants to your aid.
JUGGERNAUT.
When you arrive to help out a crewmate in the nick of time, no matter how improbable, roll +BLOOD. On a hit, you’re there. On a 7-9, you bring some problem along with you. On a miss, you are waylaid by a familiar face or a friend in trouble.
LIKE A TWIG.
When you hit while getting into a scrap, you may mark a trouble to break (choose 1):
their weapon

GOOD SAMARITAN.
When you protect a crewmate, refresh your bonds with them.
STORMBLOODED.
When you have the Raging trouble and give into its compel, choose two that happen immediately:
  • You mark all-luck.
  • Your weapons and fists are 1-wreck until you take a breather.
  • You summon a great thunderstorm around you; describe how it aids you.
CHANNEL.
At character creation, mark 2 dark powers. For every 1 bond you have with your demon, mark an additional power. When you lose a bond with your demon, lose a power. When you channel a dark power, spend 1-luck and roll +SPITFIRE. On a hit, unleash 1 dark power you’ve marked. On a 7-9, suffer 1-harm as well. On a miss, take 3-harm to channel the power, or else the demon can do whatever it wants for a few seconds. The powers last a scene.
Shapeshift into anything you’ve seen that’s about your size or smaller.
Create fireballs and infernos around you (dangerous fire area loud 2-harm).
Animate a corpse (bound slow 2-harm close) from a nearby shadow.
Force someone in your sight, whose name you know, to follow your next order (it can’t harm them).
Cloak yourself in shadow for strange hardy 2-armor.
See the deep desires and dark secrets of everyone around you.
Enter the dreams of a person whose name you know, and give them visions.
BARGAIN.
When you bargain with your demon for something, spend a bond with them and roll +POLISH. On a hit, the demon will offer you a deal with a catch, and demand something from the list of sacrifices. Take the deal or leave it. On a 10+, you have wiggle room; bargain to remove the catch or lessen the sacrifice. On a miss, take Bedeviled or, if you already have it, you’re compelled to take a terrible bargain the demon offers.
CURSE.
You gain the following option when you twist fate: • Your enemy receives a stroke of strange bad luck; what must they do to recover?
GAMBLER’S EDGE.
Mark 1-luck every time you end a session with Bedeviled.
INFERNAL CONTRACT.
When you create a contract with someone to give them some of the demon’s power, state the terms and roll +max bonds with your demon. On a 7-9, both of you hold 1; on a 10+, both of you hold 2. Both of you can spend 1 hold to channel without rolling. On a miss, or if they break the contract, you and they both take 1-harm and mark Bedeviled. Whenever they give into the demon’s desires willingly, you can sense it, and both of you hold 1.
INFERNAL ROTES.
When you take this move, pick a dark power you already have. By grasping your dark token, you can spend 2-luck to use that power without having to channel.
THE CONTRACT OF POWER.
Mark a dark power. You don’t have to spend 1-luck the first time you channel during a session.
THE CONTRACT OF GREED.
Mark a dark power. When you channel, you can spend 1 bond with a watching crewmate—instead of 1-luck—to trigger the move.
SHEPHERD.
You are the shepherd of this ship. When you make a move while on your ship, you may spend 1-luck to use +max bonds with your ship instead of the move’s trait.
In addition, at character creation, mark one of the following moves.
AUGURY.
When you meet an important character for the first time, you may give the Fates one prediction about their (choose 1):
past
secrets
goals
The Fates will immediately tell you if this prediction is true or not. If it’s true, take 1-luck; if it’s not true, the Fates will tell you why it isn’t.
COMMUNE.
When you commune with a leviathan, become Hunted and roll +SPITFIRE. On a hit, choose 1. On a 10+, choose 2. The leviathan will:
return to the deeps
turn its ire on your enemies
permit you passage
impart secret knowledge to you
The Fates will tell you what danger its presence brings. On a miss, the leviathan is, or brings, the sea’s servants.
BOSUN’S BLESSING.
When you twist fate while steering your ship, you may spend bond with your ship, 1-for-1, instead of luck. If you do, you can choose from the following additional effects:
  • You spot something hidden just beneath the waves; what is it and how could it aid you?
  • You notice a way to take cover and lose pursuers; what is it and how could you take advantage?
  • The weather turns against your enemies; what happens and how does it mess them up?
At any time, you may mark Hunted to act as if you’ve spent 2-luck on twisting your fate, and pick one of the above options.
READ THE STARS.
When you read the stars on a clear night at the prow of your ship, spend 1-luck and roll +SPITFIRE. On a hit, the stars tell you 1, and the Fates answer the attached question. On a 10+, learn 2, or learn 1 and answer the attached question yourself. On a miss, you must mark Hunted to learn 1.
  • A way into something, a way out of something, or a way home. Where to?
  • A secret only the stars have heard before. Which secret?
  • The fate of something: where their heart’s compass points. Whose fate?
STAR BLESSING.
Spend x-luck to bless a crewmate. That crewmate has strange 1-armor that they may use x times before it disappears; it can be used multiple times a session.
SUMMANUS REIGNS.
You may unleash your powers to control the weather. If you miss, take Hunted in addition to the other consequences.
TRAINED EYE.
When observing a new ship, roll +VINEGAR. On a hit, you know two of the following, your choice: its wreck tally, its captain and crew size, its weaponry, its hidden tricks, and a rumor about its weaknesses. On a 10+, you know all of it. On a miss, you know the ship all too well; the Fates will tell you who you’re sure is on this vessel, and why they hate your guts
SCAMP.
You have a nemesis and a protector. Any time your nemesis stops you from causing trouble for the crew, they mark 1-luck. Anytime your protector successfully gets you out of trouble, they refresh all bonds with you. At the beginning of every chapter, ask your crewmates: which of you is most pissed at me right now? Which of you is most likely to stick their neck out for me? The first becomes your new nemesis; the second becomes your new protector.
At character creation, mark two of the following effects. You can spend 1 bond with your nemesisor protector, or take Greedy, to use any marked effects on them.
Sneak up on them, sneak past them, or eavesdrop on them.
Know exactly what secret or weakness they’re hiding.
Bait them, and compel them to do something dumb in response.
Nick something from them without them noticing.
In addition, at character creation, mark one of the following moves.
CHARY CATECHUMEN.
You may spend 1-luck to make someone who isn’t on the crew your new nemesis until the end of the scene. Whenever you save your protector from the machinations of your nemesis, refresh all your bonds
CHEEKY.
Mark an additional Scamp effect that you can use. Whenever you use a Scamp effect, mark 1-luck.
EVERLASTING ARSENAL.
You always have another 1-harm knife that you can pull out of an increasingly unlikely place.
GOOD FOR THIEVIN’.
When you squeeze into somewhere others can’t, roll +BLOOD. On a hit, tell the Fates where you get to. On a 10+, you may find a secret that you didn’t expect, if you want it. On a miss, someone or something else is in there with you.
LIGHTFOOT.
When you sneak around without anyone noticing, roll +VINEGAR. On a hit, hold 1. On a 10+, hold 2. Spend hold to sneak past someone, even if they’re looking for you. On a miss, take a trouble to hold 1, or get caught.
ROB.
When you rob something under someone’s nose, explain what your opening is and roll +VINEGAR. On a hit, you nick something you want. On a 7-9, you’re compelled to take more, even though it’ll get you in trouble. On a miss, you’re caught and they’re pissed.
SABOTEUR.

BELONGINS
DOWNTIME MOVES
BEGIN DOWNTIME.
When you enter port to rest awhile or roll 10+ while navigating, and you aren’t currently on an adventure, begin a downtime. If you are being actively hunted, you must pay 1-treasure in a port to take downtime, to avoid the watchful eyes of your pursuers. (The Fates may adjust this cost depending on the port and pursuer.) If you stay in a port for more than one downtime, spend 3-treasure for each extra downtime you take, to cover your expenses.
LEAVE DOWNTIME.
When you begin a new adventure, leave port, or reach a destination, finish your downtime and heal all harm. This recuperation only covers wounds taken during your previous adventures. If you take fresh harm during your downtime activities, you can’t remove that harm using this move.
MAKE DUBIOUS FRIENDS.
When you make a dubious friend during downtime, roll +POLISH. On a hit, tell the Fates what small favor they’re willing to do for you. On a 7-9, the Fates will tell you what they expect of you in return. On a miss, they give you a trouble; clear it when you do what they want.
CAROUSE.
When you carouse during downtime, spend 1-treasure and roll +POLISH. On a hit, describe how you’re enjoying yourself; mark 3-luck in your abandon. On a 10+, tell the Fates whether you made shady friends or caught wind of an opportunity; describe it. On a miss, mark 1-luck, but you overindulge in your weaknesses; mark your playbook’s vice.
TEST YOUR BONDS.
When you test your bonds with a crewmate during downtime, tell them what you’re confronting them about, spend x bond with them, and roll +x. On a hit, you’ve moved them; if either (or both) of you offers a moment of vulnerability to the other, the person who made that offer may remove one of their troubles or tags, and explain why their bonds helped them heal. On a 10+, if you choose to reveal your concealed feelings or darkest secrets, you also gain 1 bond with your crewmate. On a miss, your bonds fail you; their player tells you what you did wrong, and the Fates give you a trouble.
GAMBLE.
When you gamble during downtime, roll +SPITFIRE. On a hit, take 2-treasure in winnings; take an additional 1-treasure if you piss someone off. On a 10+, decide whether you stumble on a secret or meet an important person while gambling. On a miss, pick 1:
  • You owe someone 1-treasure; if you don’t pay up, you’re in their debt.
  • You cheat to get 1-treasure, but choose 1 unwanted attention from testing renown.
INVESTIGATE.
When you investigate something during downtime, roll +VINEGAR. On a hit, ask 2. On a 10+, ask 3 instead.
  • What can I learn about ______?
  • Where can I find ____?
  • How could I get _____ to ______?
  • What are the hidden dangers if I _____?
