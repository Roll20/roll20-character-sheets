Eccentricity & Occupation

Roll a number of d10s equal to rank, keep the highest. At rank 0, roll 2d10 and keep the lowest. A natural 10 is a Total Success. Clicking a roll button asks for an optional dice modifier (bonus dice kept highest, penalty dice kept lowest), then a Target Number (enter 0 to skip) -- the Margin row shows Roll minus TN: 0 or higher is a success. Dice pools never exceed 7d10 (soft cap) or 10d10 (hard cap).
