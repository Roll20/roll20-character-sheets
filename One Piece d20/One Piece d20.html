Health

LETHAL / NONLETHAL
Damage-mode comparison

These are alternative conventions in the book, not two damage pools to apply at once. Use the selected convention; the other tracker is retained but not interpreted. Current HP never changes automatically when stats or maximum HP change.

Base maximum = level × (5 + CON modifier + 2 per Blooded purchase + other HP/level), plus the flat adjustment. “Fill” is a manual setup/healing tool, not a rest rule; it also clears accumulated nonlethal damage. Death threshold is shown as −½ maximum HP without adding a rounding rule.

Attacks & techniques

ROLL • RESOLVE • DAMAGE

Add a row for each weapon, unarmed strike, or special attack. Use the Attack button once per attack and choose its sequence penalty. Only make additional attacks you are entitled to. The row’s skill supplies rank + ability + skill adjustments; size and the global attack modifier are included once.

Attack · Damage + ·

Dice entries accept NdM and whole numbers joined by + or −. Use STR ×1.5 only when the two-handed rule applies; choose the appropriate damage attribute yourself. Fraction rounding defaults down as an adjustable implementation convention, not a quoted rule. Off-hand damage is manual because pp.158 and 163 disagree.

Fuel, ammunition & supplies

Cyborg fuel is manual. The book uses ½ bottle/day and Low on Fuel at ½ bottle or less. Cyborgs replace original racial traits but keep original size options and movement; enter those retained movement values on Combat.

Ship register

VESSEL / CREW RECORD

Choose a hull preset or use Custom / Other. Ship level and XP are independent of the character. Leveling grants ship feats, not automatic character-style HP or DR. Changing the hull, materials, or level never refills current HP.

Cannons, torpedoes & weapons

BOOK P.131

Cannon operators use their Ranged Shot skill (p.131). Enter the operator’s total, or select This character to use this sheet’s calculated Ranged Shot. Weapon statistics, reloads, targeting, and special effects remain editable. For a standard cannon, the book lists 4d6, 200 yards, and a standard-action reload. Rolls do not spend ammunition or fuel.

Attack · · ·

Slots are bookkeeping, not automatic installation approval. Large/fat/sniper and other restricted weapons still require the appropriate hull. Use 0 slots only for an approved exception such as a beam in the Soldier Dock System. Each button rolls one weapon; a group count does not multiply attacks or damage.

Rooms & upgrades

BASIC / ADVANCED / CUSTOM

Record individual rooms, basic parts, coatings, engine systems, and Soldier Dock channels. Installation, fuel spending, and bonuses are manual; place numerical effects in the hull adjustments or station totals. This keeps custom upgrades editable.

Klabautermann & ship feats

SHIP-ONLY ADVANCEMENT

Klabautermann progression uses the character XP table, with a maximum ship level of 20 (p.132). One feat is awarded per level-up; this counter assumes no starting feat and provides an allowance adjustment. These feats do not spend character feat points. Record effects here and apply numerical bonuses manually in the relevant ship fields.
