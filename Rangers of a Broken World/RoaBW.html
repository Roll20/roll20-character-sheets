CASTING A SPELL
Step 1: Plan
Combine Personal Spell Note + Terrain Spell Notes
Ask the Conductor what Spell Notes are available, and see what combinations you can create. You can use one of your Blur Notes instead of a Personal Spell Note, but you’ll gain a point of Blur if you do!
Step 2: Negotiate
Let the Conductor know what you intend for the spell to do. The Conductor proposes a Scatter cost.
Step 3: Cast
If you pay the cost, the spell happens.
CHORD
To cast a Chord, all the Rangers taking part must be acting at the same time and close to one another (a few steps away at most).
  • Combine words from all Rangers on the Chord, folowing the Casting a Spell steps.
  • Share the Scatter cost.

Backlash When you gain Blur.
Who Am I?
As you gain Blur, you feel closer and closer to the people you know. You might take on their thoughts, their feelings, and their mannerisms as your Blur increases.<br><br>At 3 Blur, you might start doing impressions unconsciously, mimicking people, or even repeating things your friends have said as you feel the line between you and them grow hazy. It’s not a joke—you’re just trying to sort out who is who, and which one is you.<br><br>At 5 Blur, you lose track of which person was you. You might change your demeanor, your voice, and manner of speaking to match someone else you know, or you might flip rapidly between friends, searching for the identity that was yours to begin with. You lose track of your memories, substituting memories you assume others to have. Until this pool is emptied, your identity is lost.
The Waiting Dead
The dead know you will join them some day. The more you gain Blur, the closer you get to them, and the closer they get to you.<br><br>At 3 Blur, the world begins to grow cold and distant and your vision fades to black and white. You might wonder what the point is, or start to feel a lurking hopelessness gnawing at you. You might struggle to take action, feeling that you’re doomed regardless of anything you do.<br><br>At 5 Blur, you become as the dead. The world becomes cold, your senses go dim, and you are flooded by the realization that it’s all over now. You lose your will to fight, as the urge to lay down your weapon and rest creeps over you. Until this pool is emptied, you can no longer engage in a Crisis.
Heedless Hurricane
The storm does not choose where it blows. As you gain Blur, a well of aggression bubbles up inside you.<br><br>At 3 Blur, you might notice your temper rising. Maybe things you could usually take in stride start to genuinely upset you, or you start feeling less patient, a little hastier. You might even feel like the people you love are beginning to grate on your nerves.<br><br>At 5 Blur, the aggression overflows. You do not choose the targets you lash out against, but you do choose the manner. You might channel this rage into combat if there are enemies available, or you might say cruel and thoughtless things to your friends. Until this pool is emptied, you lose control of your anger.
Roots
As you gain Blur, pieces of you are absorbed by the world around you.<br><br>At 3 Blur, you might hesitate and look back, reluctant to leave the pieces of yourself that are in the scenery. You might identify more with the place and less as your own person, impacted by the place’s weather and mood in a way that is difficult to describe.<br><br>At 5 Blur, you are part of the land, so deeply connected that you refuse to leave. This place contains too much of who you are, and you cannot really tell where you begin and the rest ends. You might lose track of which sensations are affecting your body and which are affecting the world around you. Until this pool is emptied, you are an inseparable part of the scenery.
The Curse of Nobility
The difference between Noble Demons and other monstrous inhabitants of this world is twofold. They are massively powerful, but they are also debilitated by intense obsessions. They hoard objects, they seek stories, they desire company—whatever means anything to them means everything to them. The more you gain Blur, the deeper your own obsessions become.<br><br>At 3 Blur, your priorities tend to get tangled. You will start pursuing your own interests or obsessions over things you know must come first, and it will be difficult for you to consciously change your priorities.<br><br>At 5 Blur, you lose yourself in a particular obsession or passion and become completely incapable of seeing beyond it to practical, even life-or-death matters like an ongoing Crisis. Until this pool is emptied, you are absorbed completely in minutiae.
Only Perfection
The stars, in their clarity and certainty, have sharpened your focus and taken the rough edges off your human nature.<br><br>At 3 Blur, you might find yourself growing stubborn, refusing to compromise, or making ambitious choices because practical options are starting to become unthinkable.<br><br>At 5 Blur, you cannot settle for anything less than perfection, and you cannot improvise. Until this pool is emptied, you lose the ability to make Adaptability rolls and to take Edge Successes.
The Wild Speech
You understand animals and they understand you. The more you gain Blur, however, the less other human beings understand you.<br><br>At 3 Blur, you might find your words getting jumbled around, or your brain blanking on how to express concepts.<br><br>At 5 Blur, you lose the ability to comprehend human language. You cannot speak any language, write, or use any sign language, though you can try your luck with gestures. Until this pool is emptied, there will always be a barrier between you and your fellow humans.
